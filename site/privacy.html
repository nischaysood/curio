Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

Curio is a learning app. This policy describes exactly what it collects, why, who else sees it, and how to have it deleted. It is written to be read rather than to be defensible.

The short version

Curio collects your email address if you create an account, the topics you ask it to teach, and your progress through lessons. It contains no advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, and no tracking across other apps or websites. Your data is never sold.

What is collected

DataWhyWhen
Email address To identify your account so your courses follow you to a new device Only if you create an account
Password To sign you in. Stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash — it cannot be read back, including by us Only if you create an account
Topics you type To generate the course you asked for Each time you request a course
Answers to teach-back questions Sent for grading, then discarded — not stored against your account When you submit a written answer
Lesson progress and daily counts To restore your place, and to apply free-tier limits Only if you create an account
Purchase status To know whether you have an active subscription Only if you subscribe

Curio does not collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, or device advertising identifier. It requests no runtime permissions beyond internet access.

Using Curio without an account

You can generate and complete courses without signing up. In that case no email is collected and no progress is stored on our servers — the topics you request are still sent to be turned into a course, but they are not linked to any identity.

Who else processes your data

Curio uses a small number of services to operate. Each receives only what it needs:

ServiceReceivesPurpose
Cloudflare Your requests, including topics Runs the Curio API
Groq The topic text and teach-back answers Generates and grades course content
Neon Email, password hash, progress Database
RevenueCat Your Curio account identifier and purchase status Manages subscriptions
Google Play Billing Payment details, handled entirely by Google Processes payment

Curio never receives your card details. Payment is handled by Google Play and we see only whether a subscription is active.

Where data is stored

Account data is stored in Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1, via Neon). Requests are processed at Cloudflare edge locations, which may be anywhere in the world, including outside your country of residence.

How long it is kept

  • Account data — until you ask for deletion.
  • Sign-in sessions — 90 days, then they expire automatically.
  • Generated courses — cached up to 90 days and shared between users, because a course on a topic is identical for everyone. Cached courses are not linked to who requested them.
  • Teach-back answers — not retained after grading.

Your choices

You can sign out from the profile screen at any time. To have your account and all associated data permanently deleted, email nischay02sood@gmail.com from the address on the account. Deletion is completed within 30 days and cannot be undone.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct or export your data, or to object to its processing. The same address handles those requests.

Children

Curio is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes. Session tokens are stored only as hashes on the server, so a breach of the session table would not allow anyone to sign in as you. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected users if a breach occurs.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above will be updated and the change noted in the app's release notes.
