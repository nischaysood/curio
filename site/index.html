How it works

  1. Type what you want to learn. Anything. Three words is enough.
  2. Curio writes the course. It plans lessons, then fills each one with material tagged by what kind of idea it is — a definition, a sequence, a comparison.
  3. That shape picks the exercise. A sequence becomes a reordering task; a definition becomes matching pairs. The teaching format follows the content, instead of everything collapsing into multiple choice.
  4. You do the work. Tap, sort, arrange, explain. Then teach the idea back in your own words.

Free and premium

Curio is free to use: one course, three lessons a day, one new course a day. Premium removes the daily limits and adds unlimited courses. Generating a course costs real money to run, which is the honest reason the free tier has a ceiling rather than an artificial one.
