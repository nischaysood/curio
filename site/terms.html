What Curio is

Curio generates interactive courses on topics you choose. Course content is produced by an automated language model. It is validated for structure before it reaches you, but it is not reviewed by a human and can be wrong.

This matters, so plainly: do not rely on Curio for medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical information. It is a tool for learning and practice, not a source of authoritative fact. Verify anything that matters against a real source.
